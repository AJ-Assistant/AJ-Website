02

Run the Installer

Double-click AJ-Assistant-Setup.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway. Follow the on-screen wizard.

03

Grant Microphone Access

Windows will ask for microphone permission on first launch. Click Allow. AJ needs mic access for wake-word detection.

04

Sign In & Activate

Log in with your email. Free tier activates automatically. If you purchased PRO, your account upgrades instantly — no license key needed.

Say "Hey AJ"

📖 USAGE GUIDE

How to use AJ Assistant.

Master AJ in minutes. Here's everything you need to know to get the most out of your AI assistant.

01

Waking AJ Up

Say "Hey AJ" from anywhere on your desktop. AJ is always listening in the background. You'll hear a soft chime and see the waveform animate — AJ is ready.

> "Hey AJ, open my project folder"
02

File & Folder Control

AJ has full read/write access to your file system. Create folders, move files, rename documents, search by content — all by voice.

> "Move all PDFs from Downloads to Work folder"
03

Screen Reading

AJ can see your screen. Point it at any window and ask questions about what's on screen — emails, code, documents, spreadsheets.

> "Read this email and summarise the key points"
04

App & Browser Control

Open, close, and switch between applications. Control your browser — open URLs, search the web, fill forms — without touching the keyboard.

> "Open YouTube and search for lo-fi beats"
05

Switching AI Models

Say which model you want — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, or a local Ollama model. AJ switches instantly mid-conversation.

> "Switch to Claude and help me write this email"
06

Deep Research Mode

AJ deploys live web crawlers, scrapes documentation, and synthesizes full research reports directly into your workspace.

> "Research the top 5 React component libraries in 2026"
07

Mobile Device Control

With AJ's Android bridge, read incoming notifications, push files wirelessly, and launch mobile apps — all from your PC.

> "Push this PDF to my phone"
08

WhatsApp Automation

Schedule and dispatch WhatsApp messages autonomously. AJ bypasses the browser to send messages directly.

> "Send meeting notes to the AJ group"

Policies & Terms.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

AJ Assistant collects only what is necessary to provide the service: your email address for account management, voice commands (processed locally or via your chosen cloud AI provider), and crash/error logs for debugging. We do not sell your data to any third party.

2. Voice Data

Voice commands sent to cloud AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) are governed by their respective privacy policies. In local/offline mode, all voice data stays on your device and never leaves. We strongly recommend offline mode for sensitive tasks.

3. Screen Data

Screen capture for the "Read my screen" feature is processed entirely locally. Screenshots are never uploaded to our servers. When using cloud AI for screen analysis, only the extracted text (OCR output) is sent, not the raw image.

4. File Access

AJ requests access to your file system only when you issue a file-related command. We do not index, scan, or upload your files without explicit voice instruction from you.

5. Contact

For privacy concerns, email us at privacy@ajassistant.in
